# Mise: restaurant software landing page

An example landing page built with the `master-design-web` skill, following the restaurant walkthrough in `guidelines/identity-and-site-building.md`. Open `index.html` in a browser. There's no build step. Fonts (DM Sans, Instrument Serif) and icons (Phosphor) load from CDNs.

Mise, the restaurants, the people and the quotes are placeholders.

## How the skill was applied

- **Identity first.** One moody photo set the tone (a dark kitchen at service time). The palette came from those photos: espresso darks, lamp-glow amber, cream paper, plus herb, copper and plum accents for the feature icons. Red is kept for late tickets and 86'd dishes.
- **Type.** One sans (DM Sans) with a serif (Instrument Serif) for the menu feel and the split heading. Six sizes in total. Large headings tightened.
- **Frankenstein wireframe.**
  1. Hero with restaurant objects scattered around the heading, and the real product (kitchen display) on a tablet below.
  2. A small rotating ribbon of restaurant logos.
  3. How it works: big photos over a photo background with stronger noise, and less of a step layout.
  4. Features: cream background, graph-paper texture, no noise, tabs on a timer with a large product view and one line of text each.
  5. Testimonials without boxes.
  6. CTA: heavy tight type, left-aligned, image background.
  7. A small centered footer.
- **Motion themes, repeated.** Sections slide up over the previous one with rounded tops. The hero pulls away (copy blurs, objects drift outward) and the CTA does the reverse. Content slides in like tickets. Everything falls back to static under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The ribbon and the tab timer both have pause buttons.
