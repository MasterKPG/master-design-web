How a rebuild runs

From slop to shipped in four weeks.

  1. Day 1

    Teardown

    We record ten minutes of red-pen notes on your homepage. Free, and yours to keep even if you never hire us.

  2. Week 1

    Identity board

    Photos of your actual place, a palette taken from them, type and a voice. You approve it before we design a single section.

  3. Weeks 2 and 3

    Rebuild

    Section by section, in your stack or a plain static site. A live link every Friday so nothing surprises you.

  4. Week 4

    Launch and hand-off

    We ship it, walk you through editing it, and hand over the code. No retainer unless you ask for one.

Before you ask

Do you use AI?

For dull jobs like resizing images and checking contrast. Every decision you see on the page was made by one of us, and we'll tell you why.

Can you keep my Webflow, Framer or Next.js site?

Yes. We rebuild in whatever you already pay for. If you have nothing, we hand over a plain static site you can host for free.

What if I don't have good photos?

Priya comes and takes them. Half a day is included in the rebuild. Phone shots of the real place still beat any stock photo.
