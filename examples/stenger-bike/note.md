# Stenger Bike

- **Source:** https://stenger-bike.de (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** e-commerce (bike and e-bike shop, Shopware), German
- **Take from it:** a mega menu organised by how riders choose a bike (type, use, motor, wheel size, drivetrain, brakes) instead of by brand or SKU; each panel ends with "Zur Kategorie →" and has a close button; a three-row header (promo strip, trust strip, logo + nav)
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/ux-and-product-thinking.md`
- **Don't take:** the rest of the page is a standard shop template; the cookie modal blocks the whole page on first visit (our first two captures were unusable)

This is the most conventional site in the set. It's here for its navbar, which is what navbar.gallery featured. Exo 2 for headings and UI, red `#e82525` for the logo and add-to-cart, black footer.

## Navbar

- **Three layers:** a black promo strip (Black Friday), a light trust strip with three short proofs, then the logo left, icons right (search, wishlist, account, cart) and the category nav centred under them.
- **Mega menu by decision, not by catalogue:** hovering "E BIKES" opens a full-width white panel with the questions a rider actually asks: what type (women's, men's, step-through, for heavier riders), what for (trekking, mountain, gravel, road, S-Pedelec), which motor brand, which wheel size, which drivetrain (belt, hub gears, electronic shifting), which brakes. Group headings in bold, links in regular, no images.
- Every panel ends with "Zur Kategorie … →" so you can skip the filters, and has a × to close.
- **Phone:** the hamburger is labelled "Navigation". A word next to the icon removes any doubt.

## What to learn

- For shops with many products, build the mega menu from the customer's decision criteria. It's a filter people can browse.
- Put trust proofs in a thin strip above the nav instead of in a "why us" section nobody scrolls to.
- Label icon-only menu buttons when the audience isn't app-native.
