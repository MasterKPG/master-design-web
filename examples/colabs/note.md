# CoLabs

- **Source:** https://colabs.com.au (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page (lab and office space for bio and deep-tech startups)
- **Take from it:** cards with inverted "notch" corners where the logo, the headline and the round arrow buttons sit; a frosted pill nav that floats over the hero; microscopy close-ups as the photo language; the footer leading with an Acknowledgement of Country
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/layout-and-spacing.md`, `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/social-proof-and-logos.md`
- **Don't take:** the stat slider on phone ("6 Tn", "5 Tn", "46") repeating the same card shape three times in a row; the reCAPTCHA badge left floating over the content

Next.js. One typeface (Matter SQ) at 400 and 600, big sizes tightly tracked (-5%). Warm off-white page `#f9f8f6`, black type, and a set of saturated card colors: green, cobalt, olive, rust, sand.

## Navbar

- **Logo outside, links inside a pill.** "Naarm/Melbourne" sits as a tiny line above the logo (the city's Aboriginal name first). The links (Services ▾, Sites ▾, About ▾, Resources ▾, Programs ▾, Contact) and two social icons live in a translucent white pill that floats over the hero. Search is a separate small circle at the far right.
- The logo itself sits in a **notch**: the hero card has an inverted rounded corner cut out of its top-left, and the logo lives in that cut-out. The nav is part of the layout, not a strip laid on top.
- **Hover:** the link turns dark green and a white rounded dropdown drops under it with the sub-pages. No icons, no descriptions. Simple.
- Stays fixed and translucent as you scroll, picking up the color of whatever is behind it.
- **Phone:** logo left, a lime "Menu" pill, then a pill holding the LinkedIn and Instagram icons. A worded "Menu" button instead of a hamburger.

## Hero

- A big rounded video card (a shrimp on a sea anemone, macro) with notches: top-left for the logo, bottom-left for "Making space for transformative innovation". A round "scroll down" arrow sits under the headline.
- Right column: a circular stat card ("1st home to Vow, Cortical Labs and more") with pager dots, a photo card "See Available Lab Space →", and a green story card "Elita: The world's first pet longevity platform" that rotates through member companies.
- Science, but warm: macro nature and microscopy, not lab-coat stock.

## Notch corners (the signature)

- Every card's action lives in an **inverted corner**: the card's corner is cut with a concave curve and a black circle arrow sits in the gap. Logo, headline, buttons and arrows all use it. It turns plain cards into a recognisable system.

## Scroll and sections

- **Service cards** in a row that runs off the right edge (a carousel): Engineering Space (green), Lab Space (cobalt), Build a Lab (olive), Innovation Facilitation (rust). Each has a circular microscopy crop, a big white title, "Learn more" and a notched arrow.
- **Benefits as pills over video:** "Wet and dry lab options / Hardware and office space / Keep your IP and offset more R&D / Find your values-led community", each line its own dark translucent pill, stacked and centered, over a soft-focus green video.
- **Belief card:** rust card, "We believe in supporting better outcomes for people and the planet", the Earth in a black circle. Next to it, two testimonial cards with the quote set big over a photo of the person.
- **Case study slider:** photo left, light-blue card right with "Case study: How Quantum Brilliance overcame the infrastructure wall", pager dashes, "Read more →".
- **Join form** on cobalt: "Building what the world needs takes a village that cares. Join us." with email and first name.

## Footer

- Charcoal rounded card. It **leads with the Acknowledgement of Country** in large type, then four site addresses with phone numbers, a short link list, legal links, "© 2026 CoLabs", the logo centered at the bottom, social icons and "Brand and website by Your Creative".

## Phone

- The notch system survives: headline in a notch under the video card, arrows in notches on every card.
- Stats become a vertical pager of sand cards with dots on the right. Service cards go one per screen.

## What to learn

- An inverted-corner notch is a strong, cheap identity device. Use it for every action button so it becomes the brand's "click here" shape.
- Put the logo inside the layout (a notch in the hero) instead of on a bar.
- Choose a photo language that matches the subject's world (macro biology for a bio lab) instead of people-in-offices stock.
- What a footer leads with says what the company values.
