# This is FC88

- **Source:** https://www.thisisfc88.com (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page (B2B: upcycling old football club apparel)
- **Take from it:** football language everywhere (Intro / Game plan / Kick-off whistle / Foul whistle / Ready to kickoff?!); a loader that draws the products (shirt, bucket hat, cap) with a % counter; a live countdown hero "276 days till the next season"; a green sidebar nav with stamped CTAs; handwritten red annotations arguing with the copy; a fill-in-the-sentence contact form
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/identity-and-site-building.md`, `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/typography.md`
- **Don't take:** the cookie toast in the bottom-right covers the content and the form on every screen until dismissed; three display faces plus a body face is a lot

Webflow + GSAP (ScrollTrigger), Lenis, Barba page transitions. Schabo Condensed (all caps, tall) for headlines, Comedik (hand-lettered) for annotations and buttons, Object Sans for text. Near-black with paper grain, pitch green `#00d47a`, pale mint, a coral red for the handwriting.

## Loading

- Mint screen, a single line icon in the centre that keeps changing (shirt, bucket hat, cap: the things they upcycle), and a big percentage counting up in the corner. Then the hero's countdown rolls from 365 down to today's number.
- The loader introduces the product range before a word is read.

## Navbar (a sidebar)

- **Green column on the left:** "THIS IS FC88" crest logo, then INTRO →, GAME PLAN →, CONTACT →, SHOP (with a tiny hand-written "new") and a cap icon. Dashed lines between items like pitch markings. Big condensed type.
- **Two stamped CTAs** at the bottom: "⬇ CHECK CATALOGUE" (dark) and "☑ I WANT TO MEET" (light), with "and contribute to cool things!" under them. Then copyright and two small social icons.
- A **news ticker** runs along the top of the content ("Official upcycling licensee of the Dutch football federation", "World's first upcycled collection made of running gear").
- **"▷| SKIP INTRO"** at the top-right: the story is long and they offer a way out.
- **Phone:** crest left, a green "☰ MENU" pill right. The menu drops as a green curtain with "↓ WHERE TO?" and the same links; the shop icon animates between cap, hat and bag.

## Copy and annotations

- The page is an argument. Big condensed statements, then **red hand-lettered notes** that push back or cheer: "every season!!", a quote crossed out with "NO. WAY.", "AND THAT'S WHY WE LOVE 'EM", "RIGHT?!". Small whistle icons label the beats ("Kick-off whistle", "Foul whistle").
- A footnote-style aside: "*Did you know? That our name is a nod to the iconic 1988 Netherlands shirt — the best one ever, in our books!"
- Near the end: "Kudos for reading this long story. Need a quick recap?" The page knows it's long.

## Scroll and sections

- Steps are separated by **dashed cut lines with scissors** (upcycling = cutting and remaking), each step expandable with "MORE INFO ▾".
- A **tilted tape banner** marquee: "SHARE THIS PAGE WITH YOUR STAKEHOLDERS". For B2B, the reader often has to convince someone else, so the page asks to be forwarded.
- **Sentence form:** "Hello, my name is ___ I'm ___ at ___ in ___. I'd like to meet because ___. Please reach me at ___." with a "GET INTRODUCED" button. A form that reads like a letter.

## Footer

- Mint block with the **smiling shirt mascot** (with hearts), newsletter field with a coral send button, email and phone underlined in coral, three address columns.
- "DID YOU KNOW? This page's coloring turns off pixels, cutting your screen's energy use by 2 to 4 times!" The dark theme is justified by the brand's mission.

## What to learn

- Borrow the customer's world for every label (here, football): nav, buttons, section names, even the loader.
- Handwritten annotations over formal headlines give the page a voice and a second layer to read.
- A countdown tied to the customer's calendar ("days till the next season") is urgency that's true.
- Ask B2B readers to share the page; make the contact form a sentence.
