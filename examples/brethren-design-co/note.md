# Brethren Design Co.

- **Source:** https://www.brethrendesignco.com (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page (branding, illustration and typography studio of two brothers) with a shop
- **Take from it:** a logo that keeps changing typeface (the studio's skill shown in the nav itself); the founders' faces on the sticky CTA; a menu card that grows out of the MENU pill; the founders photographed in the brand's pink; a shop of their own fonts on the homepage
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/identity-and-site-building.md`, `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/social-proof-and-logos.md`
- **Don't take:** body copy in 13px monospace on photos; the long "Featured work" grid of licensed franchises reads as name-dropping after a while

Fonts: teeType (their own heavy display face) for headings, Geist Mono for text. Palette: bubble-gum pink, oxblood `#280005`, cream, plus whatever the illustrations bring.

## Navbar

- **Bar:** oxblood strip, "MENU +" as a pink pill on the left, the logo centred, a cart icon with a count on the right.
- **The logo changes typeface** every second or two: blackletter, a thin script, a bold geometric caps "BRETHREN". For a studio that sells custom lettering, the nav proves the skill before any section does.
- **Menu:** the pill becomes "CLOSE ×" and a cream card **grows down out of it** (frosted at first, then solid). Links fade in one by one: HOME, OUR WORK, ABOUT US, SHOP, HIRE US in big mono caps. At the bottom, an Instagram tile "JAM THE GRAM'" that flips between a dark panel and a photo of the brothers. Hover turns a link pink.
- **Sticky actions:** a round "UP" button bottom-left and a pink "LET'S DO WORK" pill bottom-right with the **two founders' faces** in it. The CTA is literally the people you'd hire.

## Hero

- A styled photo, not a mockup: a pink CRT on a pink desk with flowers and a checkered mug; the CRT screen cycles through their work (a TUPPS can, a "Design that smokes" sticker collage).
- A huge marquee "ILLUSTRATION ✦ PACKAGING ✦ DESIGN ✦ STRATEGY ✦ BRANDING" runs across the bottom of the photo in white display type.
- Under it, "BRETHREN DESIGN CO." small, "Creative allies for bold brands, in-house teams and agencies" in mono, and "EXPLORE ▾".

## Sections

- **"DESIGN THAT SMOKES"** in outlined letters with a stepped pink shadow on cream. A tagline as a typography specimen.
- **The founders** in matching pink overalls, posed with the pink CRT, one winking. "GIVE YOUR BRAND SOME BROTHERLY LOVE!" The studio name (brethren = brothers) drives the copy and the photo.
- **Featured work** on oxblood: big rounded cards (Kpop Demon Hunters, One Piece, Fortnite cards…) with a discipline line under each.
- **Services** as cream cards with a hand-drawn paperclip at the top edge, a list of four or five promises and an illustration sample.
- **Clients** in outlined logo cards, then a long quote with name and role and pager dots.
- **Shop**: their fonts and merch as product cards with price stickers. A studio homepage that sells.
- **Marquee band** "BRETHREN DESIGN CO. (B) DESIGN THAT SMOKES" before the footer.

## Footer

- "LET'S *Do* WORK!" across the width, "Do" in a hand script, then the founders over a pink monochrome pattern of their illustrations. Explore / Follow / Contact columns, "Site by Forefathers".

## What to learn

- Let the logo or nav demonstrate the craft (here, lettering that keeps changing).
- Put faces on the CTA when people are what's being sold.
- Photograph the team inside the brand's color world; it makes "about us" part of the identity.
- A menu that grows out of the button that opened it keeps the user oriented.
