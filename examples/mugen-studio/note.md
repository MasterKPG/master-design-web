# Mugen Studio (Framer template)

- **Source:** https://mugenstudio.framer.website (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page template for a design studio (the studio, people and clients are fictional)
- **Take from it:** small "living" details in the nav (local time, a work counter); a person card in the hero with availability ("2 slots open March'26") and a "Book a 15-Min Call" button; a statement that fills from grey to white as you read; a process shown as a horizontal accordion of numbered columns
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/typography.md`, `guidelines/pricing.md`
- **Don't take:** the overall look (black, Inter Display, grey/white text, logo strip, stats row) is the standard dark-agency template; the floating "Unlock from $129 / Made in Framer" badges are the template seller's, not part of the design

Framer. Inter Display 600 for headings (tight -4%), Inter/Geist for text. Black `#050505`, white, mid-grey, hairline dividers.

## Navbar

- Thin top row: "MUGEN®" left, "Toronto (CA) 12:53 AM" (a live clock) in the middle, "Our Work [6]" right, a two-line menu icon. A live clock and a count are cheap ways to make a static nav feel current.
- **Phone menu:** a black panel slides in from the right: Home, Studio, Work [6], Articles [10], Pricing, Contact in large type, "Let's Talk" with the email and a "+", the clock again, social icons, legal row.

## Hero

- The wordmark is the headline, two-tone (white "MUGEN", grey "STUDIO"), with a dark abstract 3D video behind it.
- **A person card instead of a CTA button**: photo of the project manager, an availability chip with a small bar meter ("2 slots open March'26"), "Plans start at $7,500 / m ↗", and a white "Book a 15-Min Call" button. Scarcity + a face + a small commitment.

## Scroll and sections

- **Text fill:** the approach statement starts grey and phrases turn white as you scroll. The white phrases are the point ("We perfected the art of the work.").
- **Bracketed index labels** ("[01] Projects", "[02] Why choose us", "[04] Process", "[05] Pricing") keep a long page navigable.
- **Services hover list:** five services in large grey type on the left; the active one turns white and the right side shows its image, a numbered description and chips (Front-end, Webflow, Framer, React…).
- **Process accordion:** five columns side by side; one is open, the rest show a rotated big number. Hover or scroll opens the next.
- **Pricing:** price big with "/ month" small, a Monthly/Quarterly toggle inside each card, "+" bullet lists, a custom-project card with "Get Quote" and the person card again.
- **Testimonial tabs:** quote with a portrait, then three tabs (client logo, a result like "$12M investment funding secured", a progress line that fills while that quote is shown).

## Footer

- Over a dark 3D render: newsletter field, the email big with "+", the founder's quote, the nav in big type with counts, legal and socials, then the wordmark cropped by the bottom edge.

## What to learn

- Live details (local time, counts, slots open) make a page feel run by people.
- Replace the generic hero CTA with a person card when the buyer is booking time with a human.
- Bracketed section indexes help on long pages.
- This is a template: use it for the details, not as the look.
