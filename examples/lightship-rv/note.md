# Lightship (AE.1 electric trailer)

- **Source:** https://lightshiprv.com (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page (consumer product, electric travel trailer)
- **Take from it:** a nav that turns into a floating frosted pill and swaps the wordmark for a two-star mark on scroll; a scatter of lifestyle photos that gathers into place; one giant pinned headline with photos sliding up through it; the two-star mark reused as the footer's opener
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/hero-and-scroll-motion.md`, `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/landing-pages.md`
- **Don't take:** the newsletter modal that opens a few seconds after landing and locks the page (it blocked every first capture, desktop and phone)

Swup page transitions and Lenis smooth scroll. One typeface, F37 Bolton, regular weight only (400) at all sizes, with -5% tracking on the big lines. White page, black type, deep teal announcement bar, one orange accent (zip-code field, a "+" button, the modal's close).

## Navbar

- **Over the hero:** transparent, white text. Left: a two-line menu icon, "AE.1", "Technology", "Specs". Centre: the spaced "LIGHTSHIP" wordmark with two small stars. Right: "Experience", "Build yours". A teal announcement bar above it ("Built in Colorado. Now Delivering Nationwide." + a "See the AE.1 in person" pill).
- **After the hero:** the links gather into a **floating light-grey frosted pill** narrower than the page, and the wordmark is replaced by the **two-star mark (✦✦)**. The brand shrinks to its symbol once you know where you are.
- **Phone:** the pill from the start: menu icon, "AE.1", ✦✦, "Build yours". The menu opens as a white card that drops from the pill: big links with hairline rules (AE.1, Technology, Experience, Build yours), then two small columns (Company, Events, Press & Media / Journal, Careers, Specs) and a photo at the bottom.

## Hero

- Full-bleed video of the trailer being towed through Colorado, rounded corners inset from the page edge.
- Headline split across the frame: "Born for Adventure." left, "Built in America." right, white, regular weight, no bold. A thin line along the bottom with "↓" and "Scroll to explore".

## Scroll and motion

- **Scatter that gathers:** after the hero, lifestyle photos float in a loose cloud around a central trailer photo and drift into a tidier cluster as you scroll, then the intro paragraph appears in grey. It shows the life around the product before the product's specs.
- **Pinned giant headline:** "This is the AE.1" fills the width and stays pinned at the top while photos (desert, interior, someone making coffee) slide up through the middle and stack on each other. Side notes appear left and right ("Freedom, Reimagined.", "More of What Matters.").
- **"Your home, anywhere."** ends the stack on a campfire photo with a dark pill "Explore the AE.1".
- **Feature carousel:** "Smart Systems. Smooth Journeys." over cards with a short benefit line and a "+" to open each one.
- **Full-bleed card** "The Journey Starts with a Closer Look." over a driver photo.
- **Big statement paragraph** in 48px regular, then the real team photo in the factory with "We started Lightship with a belief…".
- News carousel ("More to Discover"), the newsletter again as a section, then the **✦✦ mark large and alone** before the footer.

## Forms

- The newsletter asks for **zip code first** (orange outline) then email, because they run roadshows near you. A form that explains its own question.

## Footer

- "Go Further." with a short mission in grey on the left. Big link list with hairline rules on the right. Newsletter row, small links in two columns, social icons, finance small print, copyright.

## Phone

- The pinned headline becomes a normal heading with photos stacked under it. The scatter collapses into a vertical stack. Everything else keeps its order. The ✦✦ mark still opens the footer.

## What to learn

- Let the nav lose the wordmark for a symbol once the visitor is past the hero.
- Show the life with the product (people, meals, kids, places) before the specs. The photos sell the feeling, the cards sell the tech.
- One pinned giant headline with images moving through it is a strong product reveal.
- Don't open a modal in the first seconds. The same form as a section near the end works without blocking anyone.
