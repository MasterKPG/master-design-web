# Raw Materials

- **Source:** https://www.therawmaterials.com/hello (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page (product design company), one long page split into seven color-coded sections
- **Take from it:** a sidebar of seven colored tabs where the current section's tab grows tall (the nav doubles as a progress map); each section owns one color; a "You are now entering ( Hello ) section" bar with a "● 01/02" counter; a logo whose letters keep swapping glyphs; 3D avatars of the team as contact cards
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/color.md`, `guidelines/hero-and-scroll-motion.md`
- **Don't take:** paragraph-length statements set at 100px+ on every screen; on phone they run for many screens with no relief

Fonts: Stabil Grotesk (regular weight, at 200px for display, tight), plus a few accent faces (Right Grotesk, Klarheit, Optimistic). Cream `#f4e9e1` page and a rainbow of flat section colors: orange-red (Hello), violet (Approach), black (Work), blue (Talent), red (Careers), yellow (Contact), green (Unusual Index).

## Navbar (colored tabs)

- **Seven stacked rounded tabs** down the left: 01 Hello, 02 Approach, 03 Work, 04 Talent, 05 Careers, 06 Contact, 07 Unusual Index. Each tab is its section's color, with a tiny number and name.
- **The current section's tab stretches tall** and the others shrink to small blocks, so the sidebar shows where you are and how much of the page each part takes. A small dot inside the tall tab moves with your progress.
- The **logo "RM" keeps morphing** (R becomes K, M splits into N + bar…). It feels like the letters are raw material being reshaped.
- **Section entry bar:** a thin full-width pill in the section's color: "You are now entering ( Work ) section ● 03/01". Each block inside a section has "● 03/02" style counters in a corner.
- **Phone:** the tabs move to a strip along the bottom of the screen; the current one is wide with its name, neighbours are colored stubs. It works like a tab bar and a progress bar at once.

## Sections

- **One color per section** carried by the tab, the entry bar, the section title card and accents in the text ("Raw Materials" in orange inside Hello; "Rigor & Romance." in violet inside Approach).
- **Typography as the visual:** most screens are one statement in huge regular type split by hairline rules, like ruled paper. Small numbered pills ("01", "02") start each line.
- **3D type** for "different": letters made of glossy white tubes, coils and beads. Used once.
- **Tabs inside sections** ("One Team · Creator Led · Phase Zero · Anti Transactional") explain the model without cards.
- **Work as a list** of client rows on black, logos right-aligned; rows marked "COMING SOON" are honest about unreleased work.
- **"The (R)aw (T)alent (N)etwork"**: initials in round badges spell the network's name.
- **Unusual Index** (green): a newsletter "Issue #1 is currently being cooked. Curious? Subscribe below." with an email field and a smiley.

## Mascots / people

- Contact uses **3D avatars of the team** (puffer jackets, hats, glasses), one per type of enquiry. Friendlier than headshots and consistent with the 3D type.

## What to learn

- A sidebar can be a progress map: one tab per section, the current one grows.
- Give each section its own color and carry it through the nav, a header bar and in-text accents.
- An "entering section" bar with counters makes a long single page feel like chapters.
- 3D avatars can replace team photos when the brand is playful and you want consistency.
