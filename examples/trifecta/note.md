# Trifecta (Framer template)

- **Source:** https://trifecta.framer.media (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page template for a digital agency (agency, people and clients are fictional)
- **Take from it:** a fixed left sidebar that holds the whole "contact card" (book-a-call with a face, open slots meter, nav, phone, email, socials); a slash "/" motif used in headings, links and indexes; link hovers that roll the text up and swap it for a red copy
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/micro-interactions-and-motion.md`
- **Don't take:** the stats ("/95+ projects", "4.9/5", "95+ businesses") and stock portraits are template filler; the seller's "Template from $129 / Made in Framer" badges aren't part of the design

Framer. Geist 600 for headings with very tight tracking (-6%), Geist/Inter for text. Warm grey `#ebe8e4`, white sidebar, black, one signal red `#f23a35`.

## Navbar (a sidebar)

- **A white column fixed on the left**, about 15% of the width, with everything a visitor needs to act:
  - top: a dark "Book a 30-Min Call / with Jessica ↗" bar with her photo,
  - an availability meter (red bars) "3 open slots for August 2026",
  - nav links with a slash prefix (/Home, /About, /Case Studies with a red "/009/" count, /Blog, /Contact),
  - a barcode, phone, big email, copyright, socials, "2026" stacked,
  - a thin outer strip with the rotated "(t)rifecta®" logo and a dot-grid icon.
- **Hover:** each link **rolls**: the black text slides up out of view and a red copy slides in from below. "Book a 30-Min Call" rolls to "Ready for a chat?" and the arrow turns from ↗ to →. The slots line gains "(+1)". The logo turns red.
- **Phone:** the book-a-call bar stays pinned at the top with the face and a dot-grid button. The menu is the same sidebar as a full-height panel, with the rotated logo along its left edge.

## Hero and sections

- "Dream /Big. We'll /Deliver." at 143px, two lines, the slash as a visual beat. A red "Start a Project ↗" and a white "See our work", a rating line.
- **Slash system:** "/Services", "/Stories.", "/Pricing.", "/FAQ.", "/001/" indexes, "/Euphoria." project names, "/The team/". One character used as the brand's punctuation.
- **Chamfered corners:** images and cards have one corner cut off at 45°, echoing the slash.
- Case studies are full-bleed image cards with a result line ("Launched eCommerce store in 3 weeks – Integrated Stripe and inventory sync").
- Process on a vertical line with red step dots and red tags.

## Footer

- Dark: a contact form (name, email, message) with a full-width red "Get in touch ↗", a person card beside it ("Taylor Kim, Client Success Manager"), then /Navigation and /Legal columns.

## What to learn

- A sidebar can be the contact card: face, availability, links, email, always visible.
- A rolling text hover (slide up, replace with a colored copy) is clear feedback that costs nothing in layout.
- One punctuation mark (here "/") can be the whole identity: headings, links, indexes, corners.
- This is a template: take the mechanics, not the look or the filler stats.
