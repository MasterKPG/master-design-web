# Brand Appart

- **Source:** https://www.brandappart.com (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page (design studio for funded startups, Paris)
- **Take from it:** a left icon sidebar as the main nav, which becomes a bottom dock with labels on phone; each client shown as a 3D keychain charm; service cards that fold back as the next one arrives; a fixed "Book a call" pill with faces; "Small team, big results" with portraits rising through the giant words
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/hero-and-scroll-motion.md`, `guidelines/social-proof-and-logos.md`
- **Don't take:** the hero showreel (a Vimeo embed) showed a "We couldn't verify the security of your connection" error in our capture, so the hero was a black box; the client logo row under the headline is grey and tiny

Lenis smooth scroll. Youth (700/900) for headings with very tight tracking (-5%), PP Neue Montreal for text, IBM Plex Mono for small labels. Cream page `#fbf9ef`, near-black `#1c1715`, and saturated service colors: indigo, orange, red, brown.

## Navbar (a sidebar)

- **Left icon rail:** six rounded-square buttons (bag = works, building = industries, target = services, smiley = about us, document = journal, envelope = contact). Grey icons on light squares at rest.
- **Hover:** the square fills (white on dark sections, light on cream), the icon turns solid black, and a small label slides out ("Works"). The rail **inverts with the section** behind it, so it stays readable on black and on cream.
- **Corners as a frame:** logo top-left, "BOOK A CALL NOW" top-right, "MENU …" bottom-left, live "Paris, France 6:54 AM" bottom-right, a scroll-progress line with a dot down the right edge. The four corners hold everything; the middle is free for the work.
- **Fixed CTA pill** at the bottom centre: "Book a free discovery call" + a white "BOOK A CALL" button with two founder avatars and a phone icon.
- **Phone:** logo left, a black square menu button right. Tapping it blurs the page and raises a **bottom dock** with the same six icons, now labelled (WORKS, INDUSTRIES, SERVICES, ABOUT US, JOURNAL, CONTACT US). The sidebar becomes a tab bar where the thumb is.

## Work as keychains

- Every client gets a **3D keychain charm**: Incard's card on a ring, a "S" charm for Stables, a padlock, a key, coins. Same lighting, same black backdrop, so very different brands sit together as one collection.
- "See more work" floats keychains, app icons and stickers around the words, drifting as you scroll.
- A testimonial in indigo sits between project cards, so proof is attached to the work.

## Services as folding cards

- "What we ship. Our ways to move fast": four big cards (01 Branding, 02 Product experiences, 03 Web design, 04 Decks), each a flat brand color with a description, a testimonial with a face, a row of project thumbnails and "SEE OUR CASE STUDIES →".
- As you scroll, the current card **tips back in 3D and slides under** while the next one comes up over it. A stack you flip through, not a grid.

## Social proof

- "Trusted by +40 founders": testimonials as fanned, overlapping cards with stars and a "CONTACT SALES ☎" tag, alternating cream and taupe. Feels like a hand of cards.
- "Small team, big results": the words huge in grey, team portraits on orange gradient cards rising through the gaps.

## Footer

- "Make every pixel pay for itself!" with "BOOK A CALL ☎", a 3D charm falling past it. Five columns (Brand Appart, Industries, Services, Featured projects, Explore more) in small bold caps, social squares, copyright, and the wordmark huge and faded across the bottom.

## What to learn

- A vertical icon sidebar frees the top of the page for the hero, and it maps naturally to a bottom dock on phones.
- Give every project the same object treatment (here keychains) so a mixed portfolio reads as one brand.
- Stacked cards that fold back make a services list feel like a sequence.
- Pin your CTA as a small pill with faces, not a big banner.
