# Self Aware (studio)

- **Source:** https://selfaware.studio and its navbar.gallery entry https://www.navbar.gallery/navbar/selfaware, captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** studio homepage (one screen) + full-screen menu
- **Take from it:** a full-screen menu built as a hanging mobile sculpture (Work, Process, Info as colored tags swinging on wires); a one-screen home that's a giant serif wordmark, a hand-drawn computer and one sentence; letters that turn to outline under the cursor
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/navigation-and-footers.md`, `guidelines/micro-interactions-and-motion.md`
- **Don't take:** the "CLICK ME!" computer that, in our capture, did nothing visible when clicked; an affordance must lead somewhere

Fonts: Schola (a classic text serif) for everything. Black on white. The menu adds three flat colors (periwinkle, red-orange, green) on black.

## Home (one screen)

- **A ticker at the top** scrolls a quote endlessly, separated by a ♪: "In an age when you can do everything and anything, we help you do something." It reads like a song lyric, a manifesto in one line.
- **"SELF AWARE"** in the serif, edge to edge, as the only heading.
- **A hand-drawn CRT computer** (a looping video, the screen shows a tree drawing). On hover the screen flips to a blue "CLICK ME!".
- One sentence under it: "A design engineering studio bringing brands to life on the web for humans and agents."
- **Letters react to the cursor:** each letter you pass over switches from solid to outline for a moment. The wordmark is the toy.

## Menu (the navbar.gallery entry)

- Full-screen black overlay, a small "Self Aware" top-left and "CLOSE" top-right in tiny caps.
- The links are **tags hanging from a mobile** (thin gold wires and balance arms, like a Calder sculpture): "Work" on a periwinkle pill, "Process" on a red-orange rectangle, "Info" on a green tilted tag, each in the serif. They sway, so the menu feels physical and a little playful.
- Two wide outlined buttons at the bottom: "WORK WITH US" and "EMAIL". The practical actions stay plain and big.
- **Phone:** the same mobile, scaled into a tall black screen, with the two buttons stacked at the bottom.

## What to learn

- A menu can be a little object to play with, as long as the plain actions (contact, email) are also there in obvious buttons.
- A one-screen studio home can be wordmark + one drawing + one sentence. Let the menu carry the rest.
- Cursor-reactive letters are a cheap way to make a wordmark feel alive. Keep the effect brief so the name stays readable.
