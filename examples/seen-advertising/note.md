# Seen Advertising

- **Source:** https://seenadvertising.agency (found via navbar.gallery), captured 2026-11-05 at 1440×900 and iPhone 13 (390×664)
- **Type:** landing page (advertising agency), built in Readymag
- **Take from it:** the whole agency dressed as a shop (services are packaged products with ingredient labels and barcodes, the nav says "Products" and "Check out", the footer is a receipt); a walking receipt mascot that turns up in the hero, the menu and the footer
- **Related guideline:** `guidelines/identity-and-site-building.md`, `guidelines/micro-interactions-and-motion.md`
- **Don't take:** the product label text set at ~8px (it's decoration, but it reads as content); the page scrolls inside a Readymag container, so normal scroll behaviour and anchors are fragile

Fonts: a heavy rounded display face in uppercase for the big words, a condensed serif for everything else. Dark grocery green `#006b2d` and warm paper cream, with black-and-white line art. Paper grain on the background.

## The concept

- **An ad agency as a grocery shop.** "WELCOME ( to our Ad Store )". Services are packaged goods: Strategro (a pizza box), Aware Spark (a soda can), Performax (instant noodles). Each has an **ingredients label** ("Ingredients: Visibility, Engagement, Impact, CPM, Hype*", "*Caution: attracts attention and increases chances of becoming the talk of the town"), an "Application" paragraph, recycling and hazard symbols and a barcode.
- The joke carries real information: the ingredients list is the service's scope, the caution line is the benefit.
- Nav items are shop words: Home, About us, **Products**, **Check out**. The footer says "THANKS FOR SHOPPING WITH US." and has a barcode.

## Mascot

- A **walking receipt** with sunglasses and sneakers, drawn in black line art. It walks on the spot next to "WELCOME", appears in the menu holding a phone, and pops through the "W" of "WITH US" in the footer.
- It isn't explained or captioned. It's simply the shop's character, which is why it works.

## Navbar

- A **green pill** centered at the top with Home (active, cream fill), About us, the "SEEN" logo in 3D shadowed letters breaking out of the pill, Products, Check out. The logo is bigger than the bar holding it.
- **Phone:** "SEEN" logo left, an outlined "Menu" pill right. The menu slides in from the right as a full green panel: PRODUCTS / ABOUT US / CHECK OUT in huge type, the mascot, and Instagram/LinkedIn printed on a **torn receipt**.

## Motion

- Bracketed lines type themselves: "( t )" → "( to our Ad Store )", "( from strategy to execution )".
- Products rotate slowly (spin sequences), so a can shows its edge then its label.
- Marquee band of the promise under the hero, marquee of client names under "CUSTOMERS".
- Review tickets slide in fanned like coupons.
- Paper props (plane, bag) drift across section edges.

## Footer

- A receipt: big outlined rounded box on green, "THANKS FOR SHOPPING WITH US.", a huge "S" mark, email, phone, address, "Made by Seen 2024 ©", two round cream social buttons and a vertical barcode strip on the right edge.

## Phone

- Each product gets a full screen with the pack big and rotating, name and "Discover" above it. The concept survives intact.

## What to learn

- Pick a metaphor from outside your industry and commit to it in every label: nav words, section names, footer, form copy.
- A mascot is strongest when it lives in the UI (menu, footer, hero) instead of a "meet our mascot" section.
- Product-style "ingredients" are a fun, honest way to list what a service includes.
