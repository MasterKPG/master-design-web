# Pricing

Pricing cards, pricing pages on marketing sites, and pricing and billing pages inside SaaS apps.

## Plans

- **3-4 tiers**, ordered by commitment. Five is too many; drop at least one.
- A "most popular" badge on one plan, usually the middle.
- Show the actual discount, and what the next plan adds that this one lacks (Resend, Supabase).
- Too many plans, with names bigger than prices, is a vibe-coding tell (`vibe-coding-tells.md`).

## Pricing cards

1. **Price big, plan name small.** People care about the price, not the name.
2. Whole-number prices, with the unit (per month, per user) next to the price.
3. Put the button near the top, under the price. Keep hierarchy by making it outline-only (no fill).
4. Replace bullets with **check marks**, and list a feature this plan *doesn't* include, so the next plan looks better.
5. Give the plan a **name and a short description**.
6. Add the honest billing note (e.g. "billed annually, $20/month").
7. Separate the price block from the features with a reduced-opacity primary background, rather than a divider line.
8. An actionable CTA label that matches the offer: "Start for free", "Get started", "Talk to sales".

## Pricing pages

- A monthly/annual toggle with the saving shown.
- 4-6 features per plan, "Everything in [previous plan], plus...", and a grouped comparison table below (accordions on mobile).
- Strongest proof next to the CTAs (`social-proof-and-logos.md`); about 5 FAQs (cancellation, contracts, trials) on the page itself.
- Tooltips open on tap, not only hover. On mobile, stack the cards and keep the buy button sticky.

## Billing pages (inside the app)

- The same card rules apply to the plan picker.
- Add the billing email and payment method.
- Use tabs (Billing, Usage) so the page can grow.
